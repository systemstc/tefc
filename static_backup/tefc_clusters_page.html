Cluster Overview

Karur is known as the "Textile Capital of Tamil Nadu", renowned for its production of high-quality home textiles catering to domestic and international markets. The cluster specialises in bed linens, table linens, kitchen linens, and home decor items.

The cluster has played a pivotal role in India's export economy with strong buyer networks across the USA, UK, Germany, and the Middle East. It houses over 1,500 manufacturing units, many of which are ISO and OEKO-TEX certified.
