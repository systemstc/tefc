1. What is Certificate of Origin?

A Certificate of Origin (CoO) is an official document that certifies that the goods being exported are manufactured or processed in India. It is required by customs authorities in the importing country to determine tariff rates and eligibility under trade agreements.

2. Documents Required

  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Shipping Bill
  • Import Export Code (IEC)
  • Purchase Order / Letter of Credit

3. How to Apply

  1. Login to Trade Connect Portal
  2. Fill the CoO application form
  3. Upload required documents
  4. Submit application
  5. Download digitally signed certificate
