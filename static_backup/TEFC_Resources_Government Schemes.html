Government Schemes

Central government incentives, subsidies, and support programmes for textile exporters and manufacturers

Active

RoDTEP Scheme

Remission of Duties & Taxes on Exported Products
0.5% – 4.3% of FOB Value
Duty credit scrips transferable or usable against import duties
Covers rebate on all central & state taxes embedded in exports
Applicable to all textile HSN codes
Eligibility: All textile exporters with IEC and shipping bill
Active

PLI for Textiles

Production Linked Incentive — MMF & Technical Textiles
Up to ₹10 Crore over 5 years
15% incentive on incremental sales in years 1–2
Covers MMF apparel, MMF fabrics, and technical textiles
Investment threshold: ₹100 Cr for large, ₹10 Cr for SMEs
Eligibility: MMF & Technical Textile Manufacturers
Active

TUFS — Technology Upgradation

Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme for Textiles
15% Capital Subsidy on New Machinery
Subsidy on plant & machinery investment for textile units
Enhanced benefits for power loom and handloom sectors
Applicable to new greenfield and brownfield expansions
Eligibility: All textile manufacturing units upgrading machinery
New

PM MITRA Park Scheme

PM Mega Integrated Textile Region and Apparel Parks
₹500 Cr per Park — 7 Parks across India
World-class plug-and-play infrastructure for textile units
Common testing labs, logistics, and worker housing
Priority allotment for TEFC cluster MSMEs
Eligibility: Textile manufacturers in 7 selected states
Active

Samarth — Skill Development

Scheme for Capacity Building in Textile Sector
Free Training + ₹8,000 stipend per trainee
Placement-linked free skill training for textile workers
Covers weaving, garmenting, spinning, and technical textiles
Industry-connected curriculum with placement support
Eligibility: Unemployed youth aged 14–35 years
Limited Slots

Market Access Initiative (MAI)

Govt. Subsidy for Export Market Development
Up to 50% of trade fair expenses covered
Subsidy for participation in international trade fairs
Support for buyer-seller meets and product adaptation
Branding and market research assistance
Eligibility: Export Promotion Councils & registered MSMEs
