1. What is ECGC?

Export Credit Guarantee Corporation (ECGC) provides credit risk insurance to exporters against non-payment by overseas buyers due to commercial or political risks. It helps exporters expand confidently into global markets by safeguarding their receivables.

2. Documents Required

  • Import Export Code (IEC)
  • Business Registration Certificate
  • Export Invoices
  • Buyer Details / Credit Information
  • Shipping Documents
  • Bank Certificate

3. How it Works

  1. Exporter applies for ECGC policy
  2. ECGC assesses buyer and country risk
  3. Policy is issued to exporter
  4. Exporter ships goods to buyer
  5. If payment default occurs, claim is filed
  6. ECGC compensates the exporter as per policy
