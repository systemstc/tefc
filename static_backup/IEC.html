1. What is Import Export Certification?

Import Export Certification refers to the mandatory registrations and approvals required for businesses to legally engage in international trade. The most essential certification is the Import Export Code (IEC), issued by DGFT, which enables exporters and importers to operate globally.

2. Documents Required

  • PAN Card of Business/Individual
  • Aadhaar Card / Identity Proof
  • Business Address Proof
  • Bank Certificate / Cancelled Cheque
  • Digital Photograph

3. How to Apply

  1. Visit DGFT Portal
  2. Create/Login to your account
  3. Fill IEC Application Form
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Pay application fee
  6. Receive IEC Certificate digitally
