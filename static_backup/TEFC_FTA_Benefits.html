Why Leverage FTA Benefits

How FTAs and government schemes directly improve your export margins and market access

Reduced Duty Burden

FTA preferential rates cut import duties from 12–25% to near zero in key markets, directly improving your price competitiveness against rivals from non-FTA countries.

Expanded Market Access

Access 58+ countries across Asia, Europe, Africa & the Middle East with preferential terms, allowing you to diversify your buyer base and reduce dependence on any single market.

Faster Customs Clearance

FTA shipments with valid CoO documentation experience streamlined customs processing in partner countries, reducing transit time and demurrage charges.

Stack Multiple Benefits

Combine FTA duty advantages with RoDTEP, RoSCTL and other export incentive schemes for maximum margin improvement — subject to DGFT stacking guidelines.

Brand India Advantage

CoO documentation certifies Indian origin, allowing buyers to promote "Made in India" — a growing preference in EU, UK and Australian markets for ethical sourcing.

TEFC Advisory Support

Our cluster-based export counsellors help you identify applicable FTAs, navigate HS classification, prepare CoO documentation and file scheme applications without cost.

Government Incentive Schemes

Financial and non-financial support programmes available to textile exporters

RoDTEP
Remission of Duties & Taxes on Exported Products
Active
0.5–4.3%
Rebate rate on FOB export value
via tradeable duty credit scrips
  • Remission of embedded central, state and local taxes
  • Duty credit scrips tradeable on exchanges
  • Covers HS chapters 50–63 (all textile categories)
  • No cap on per-exporter benefit amount
Eligibility: All textile exporters with valid IEC and GST registration
PLI Scheme
Production Linked Incentive for Textiles
Active
Up to ₹10 Cr
Annual incentive on incremental production over base year
  • 15% incentive on incremental sales (years 1–2)
  • Covers MMF apparel, MMF fabrics, Technical Textiles
  • Minimum investment threshold: ₹300 Cr (large) / ₹10 Cr (MSME)
  • 5-year incentive period from commencement year
Eligibility: MMF & Technical Textile manufacturers meeting minimum investment criteria
TUFS
Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme
Active
15% subsidy
On capital investment in textile machinery upgradation
  • Capital subsidy on new textile machinery purchase
  • Covers weaving, processing, garmenting & technical textiles
  • Additional 10% for SC/ST, women-owned & NE region units
  • Interest reimbursement on term loans up to ₹2 Cr p.a.
Eligibility: All textile units upgrading approved machinery — SSI, powerloom, garmenting units
RoSCTL
Rebate of State and Central Taxes & Levies
Active
0.5–8.2%
Rebate rate on FOB value for apparel & made-ups
  • Specific to apparel (HS 61–62) & made-ups (HS 63)
  • Remission of SGST, stamp duty, mandi tax & other levies
  • Issued as tradeable e-scrips via ICEGATE portal
  • Higher rates for higher value-added garments
Eligibility: Garment & made-up exporters with valid IEC; excludes SEZ units claiming other benefits
PMEGP
PM Employment Generation Programme
Active
15–35%
Margin money subsidy on project cost (category-wise)
  • Subsidy up to ₹25 lakh for manufacturing units
  • 35% for rural SC/ST/women/minorities, 25% urban
  • Applicable for handloom, khadi & small garment units
  • Bank-linked credit programme via nationalised banks
Eligibility: Individuals 18+, self-help groups, registered societies, textile artisans & weavers
SFURTI
Scheme of Fund for Regeneration of Traditional Industries
Active
₹3–5 Cr
Grant per cluster for capacity building & infrastructure
  • Cluster development for handloom, silk & jute sectors
  • Common facility centres, design & testing labs
  • Training & skill development for artisan clusters
  • Market linkage & branding support for traditional textiles
Eligibility: Clusters with min. 500 artisans in traditional textile sectors; institutional implementing agencies

Note: Scheme rates and eligibility criteria are subject to periodic revision by the Ministry of Textiles and DGFT. Exporters are advised to verify current rates at the official DGFT portal (dgft.gov.in) or contact their nearest TEFC cluster office before filing applications. TEFC does not guarantee approval of scheme benefits.

Important: To claim FTA benefits, you must submit a valid Certificate of Origin (CoO) from an authorised issuing body. The Textiles Committee is the designated CoO issuing authority for textiles under most FTAs. Apply for CoO at your nearest TEFC cluster office or online at textilescommittee.gov.in. FTA benefits cannot be claimed without a valid CoO even if the goods qualify.

Certificate of Origin (CoO) — How to Apply

CoO is mandatory to claim preferential duties under all FTAs. The Textiles Committee is the authorised issuing body for textile CoOs.

1
Register on TC Portal

Create your exporter account on textilescommittee.gov.in with IEC, GST and bank details.

2
Submit Shipment Details

Enter invoice, packing list, HS code, buyer details and target country FTA.

3
Document Verification

TEFC / TC officer verifies documents within 1–2 working days. Physical inspection may apply.

4
CoO Issuance

Digitally signed CoO issued and downloadable from portal. Also couriered if physical copy required.
