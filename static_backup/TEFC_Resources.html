Export Process

A step-by-step guide to exporting textiles from India — from registration to realisation

1

Exporter Registration & IEC

Registration

Before you can export, obtain an Import Export Code (IEC) from DGFT. This is a 10-digit code mandatory for all exports. Also register on the ICEGATE portal for customs purposes.

  • Apply for IEC on DGFT portal (dgft.gov.in)
  • Register your firm as an exporter with FIEO or relevant EPC
  • Open a designated foreign currency account with your bank
  • Register on ICEGATE for customs filing (icegate.gov.in)
Download IEC Application Guide (PDF)
2

Export Documentation

Documentation

Prepare all mandatory export documents before shipment. Incomplete documentation is the most common cause of delays and payment failures in textile exports.

  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (CoO) — preferably from Textiles Committee for FTA benefits
  • Shipping Bill / Bill of Export
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • GST LUT (Letter of Undertaking) for zero-rated exports
Download Document Checklist (PDF)
3

Quality Certification & Compliance

Compliance

Many importing countries require quality and safety certifications for textile products. Getting the right certifications opens new markets and commands better prices.

  • OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for chemical safety
  • ISO 9001 for quality management systems
  • BIS certification for domestic standard (if applicable)
  • Buyer-specific quality audits (BSCI, SEDEX, Higg Index)
  • Textiles Committee quality certificate for CoO
Download Certification Guide (PDF)
4

Customs Clearance & Shipping

Shipping

File the shipping bill on ICEGATE, arrange customs examination if required, and coordinate with CHA (Customs House Agent) for smooth clearance at the port.

  • File Shipping Bill on ICEGATE portal
  • Submit documents to customs via CHA
  • Let Export Order (LEO) issued after customs approval
  • Load goods onto vessel / aircraft after LEO
  • Obtain Bill of Lading from shipping line
Download Customs Guide (PDF)
5

Payment Realisation & Incentives

Payment

Realise export proceeds within the stipulated time (currently 9 months for general exports, 15 months for project exports). Claim all applicable benefits and incentives.

  • File EBRC (Export Bank Realisation Certificate) with bank
  • Claim RoDTEP scrips after realisation
  • File IGST refund claim on GST portal
  • Claim drawback if applicable
  • Update DGFT on export performance for MEIS/RoSCTL (if applicable)
Download Incentive Claims Guide (PDF)

Common Mistakes

  • Missing CoO for FTA-route exports
  • Wrong HS code on shipping bill
  • Delay in GST LUT renewal
  • Not registering AD Code at port
  • Late EBRC filing causes RoDTEP delay

Government Schemes

Central government incentives, subsidies, and support programmes for textile exporters and manufacturers

Active

RoDTEP Scheme

Remission of Duties & Taxes on Exported Products
0.5% – 4.3% of FOB Value
Duty credit scrips transferable or usable against import duties
Covers rebate on all central & state taxes embedded in exports
Applicable to all textile HSN codes
Eligibility: All textile exporters with IEC and shipping bill
Active

PLI for Textiles

Production Linked Incentive — MMF & Technical Textiles
Up to ₹10 Crore over 5 years
15% incentive on incremental sales in years 1–2
Covers MMF apparel, MMF fabrics, and technical textiles
Investment threshold: ₹100 Cr for large, ₹10 Cr for SMEs
Eligibility: MMF & Technical Textile Manufacturers
Active

TUFS — Technology Upgradation

Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme for Textiles
15% Capital Subsidy on New Machinery
Subsidy on plant & machinery investment for textile units
Enhanced benefits for power loom and handloom sectors
Applicable to new greenfield and brownfield expansions
Eligibility: All textile manufacturing units upgrading machinery
New

PM MITRA Park Scheme

PM Mega Integrated Textile Region and Apparel Parks
₹500 Cr per Park — 7 Parks across India
World-class plug-and-play infrastructure for textile units
Common testing labs, logistics, and worker housing
Priority allotment for TEFC cluster MSMEs
Eligibility: Textile manufacturers in 7 selected states
Active

Samarth — Skill Development

Scheme for Capacity Building in Textile Sector
Free Training + ₹8,000 stipend per trainee
Placement-linked free skill training for textile workers
Covers weaving, garmenting, spinning, and technical textiles
Industry-connected curriculum with placement support
Eligibility: Unemployed youth aged 14–35 years
Limited Slots

Market Access Initiative (MAI)

Govt. Subsidy for Export Market Development
Up to 50% of trade fair expenses covered
Subsidy for participation in international trade fairs
Support for buyer-seller meets and product adaptation
Branding and market research assistance
Eligibility: Export Promotion Councils & registered MSMEs

Important: To claim FTA benefits, you must submit a valid Certificate of Origin (CoO) from an authorised issuing body. The Textiles Committee is the designated CoO issuing authority for textiles under most FTAs. Apply for CoO at your nearest TEFC cluster office or online at textilescommittee.gov.in. FTA benefits cannot be claimed without a valid CoO even if the goods qualify.
