Online Services

Access all TEFC digital services and government portals from one place. Submit applications, track status, register for training, apply for certifications and more — all online, all paperless.

TEFC Digital Services

Certificate of Origin
  • Official document certifying that a product was manufactured or processed in India.
  • Mandatory for most international shipments to clear customs at the destination.
  • Available in both Non-Preferential (standard) and Preferential (trade agreement) formats.
  • Issued by authorized bodies like the Textiles Committee or Chambers of Commerce.
  • Can be applied for and issued electronically through the DGFT portal.
Export Credit Guarantee (ECGC)
  • Provides insurance to exporters against the risk of non-payment by foreign buyers.
  • Protects against commercial risks (insolvency) and political risks (war/trade bans).
  • Makes it easier for exporters to secure pre-shipment or post-shipment bank loans.
  • Enables confident entry into new or high-risk international markets.
  • Ensures business continuity by compensating for losses from payment failures.
Letter of Credit
  • A guarantee from the buyer’s bank that payment will be made once terms are met.
  • Acts as one of the most secure payment methods in international trade.
  • Payment is triggered only upon submission of proof of shipment and required papers.
  • Governed by international rules (UCP 600) to ensure global uniformity.
  • Bridges the trust gap between Indian sellers and foreign buyers.
HS Code (Harmonized System)
  • A 6-to-8-digit standardized numerical method used to classify products worldwide.
  • Determines the exact customs duty and GST rates applicable to textile goods.
  • Ensures correct product identification globally, regardless of language barriers.
  • Essential for filing shipping bills and avoiding legal delays at customs.
  • Helps exporters check eligibility for specific government incentives or schemes.
IEC (Import Export Code)
  • A unique 10-digit identification number mandatory for all Indian export businesses.
  • Granted by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Ministry of Commerce.
  • Valid for a lifetime, subject to a simple annual online update.
  • Required for clearing shipments and making/receiving foreign currency payments.
  • Fully digital application process linked directly to the business’s PAN card.
