Textile Industry & Standards (50 FAQ Highlights)

What is the Jute Mark India (JMI) scheme?

JMI is a certification mark that provides an assurance of quality and origin for jute products manufactured in India.

How do I apply for Handloom Mark?

You can apply through the Textiles Committee portal by submitting your looms details and fabric samples for testing.

What are Technical Textiles?

These are textiles manufactured primarily for their technical performance and functional properties rather than aesthetic characteristics (e.g., medical, agro-textiles).

Which lab tests are mandatory for cotton export?

Common tests include GSM, Color Fastness, Tensile Strength, and Shrinkage tests as per ISO or AATCC standards.

Is organic certification required for textiles?

If you are marketing as 'Organic', GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard) certification is generally required for international markets.

Export Procedures & Policies (50 FAQ Highlights)

What is the RoDTEP Scheme?

Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products provides a rebate on central/state taxes not otherwise refunded.

How to obtain an IEC (Import Export Code)?

You must apply online through the DGFT website using your PAN and bank details.

What is a Certificate of Origin (COO)?

A document declaring in which country a commodity was manufactured. Crucial for claiming FTA benefits.

What are FTA benefits for Indian textiles in Australia?

Under the ECTA, most Indian textile products enjoy zero-duty access to the Australian market.

What is a Letter of Credit (LC)?

A payment mechanism where the buyer's bank guarantees payment to the exporter upon submission of valid shipping documents.
