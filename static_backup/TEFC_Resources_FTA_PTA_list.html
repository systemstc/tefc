Important: To claim FTA benefits, you must submit a valid Certificate of Origin (CoO) from an authorised issuing body. The Textiles Committee is the designated CoO issuing authority for textiles under most FTAs. Apply for CoO at your nearest TEFC cluster office or online at textilescommittee.gov.in. FTA benefits cannot be claimed without a valid CoO even if the goods qualify.
