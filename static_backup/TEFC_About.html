Overview

Who we are, what we do, and why we exist

What is TEFC?

The Textiles Exports Facilitation Centre (TEFC) is a strategic initiative by the Textiles Committee, Ministry of Textiles, Government of India. Established to serve as a one-stop support hub for MSMEs and exporters in the textile sector, TEFC provides essential market intelligence, trade certification, and quality assurance to help Indian textile businesses compete effectively on the global stage.

TEFC operates through a network of six strategically located centres across major textile manufacturing hubs in India — Karur, Surat, Ichalkaranji, Jaipur, Varanasi, and Ludhiana. Each centre offers tailored services that address the specific needs of the local textile cluster, while collectively working toward a unified national vision of scaling India's textile exports to ₹100 billion by 2030.

As a nodal body for export facilitation, TEFC bridges the gap between government policy and ground-level implementation, ensuring that even the smallest textile MSME can access the benefits of Free Trade Agreements, government incentive schemes, and international buyer networks.

Mission, Vision & Objectives

The principles that guide every decision, programme, and service at TEFC

Our Mission
Why we exist

To facilitate and strengthen the export capabilities of India's textile MSMEs by providing end-to-end support in market access, trade compliance, quality certification, and policy navigation — making global trade accessible to every textile entrepreneur.

  • Bridge the gap between policy and on-ground export readiness
  • Deliver market intelligence for informed export decisions
  • Enable MSMEs to leverage FTA benefits effectively
  • Provide certification support for quality & sustainability
Our Vision
Where we are headed

To position India as the world's foremost destination for high-quality, sustainable, and competitively priced textiles — empowering over 45 million people employed in the sector by creating a globally connected, technologically enabled, and policy-supported export ecosystem.

  • India's textile exports to reach $100 billion by 2030
  • Every cluster connected to international buyer networks
  • Sustainability and compliance as core export strengths
  • TEFC as the global benchmark for textile trade facilitation
Our Objectives
Strategic priorities
  • Provide single-window facilitation for all export documentation, certifications, and compliance
  • Disseminate real-time market intelligence on global textile demand, pricing, and buyer requirements
  • Build MSME capacity through training in quality management, sustainable practices, and export operations
  • Facilitate buyer-seller meets, trade fair participation, and international delegations
  • Assist exporters in availing RoDTEP, PLI, TUFS, and other government incentive schemes
  • Promote adoption of digital tools, e-commerce, and technology in export operations

About Us

The Textiles Export and Facilitation Centre (TEFC) is a strategic initiative by the Textiles Committee, Ministry of Textiles, Govt. of India. We serve as a one-stop support hub for MSMEs and exporters in the textile sector, providing essential market intelligence, trade certification, and quality assurance to help Indian textile businesses compete globally.

Led by an experienced team of trade experts, industry professionals, and government officials, TEFC brings together deep sector knowledge with administrative capability to deliver measurable outcomes for textile exporters across all six clusters. Our leadership is committed to a transparent, accessible, and impactful facilitation ecosystem.

The Textiles Committee — our parent body — is a statutory organisation established under the Textiles Committee Act, 1963, and has over six decades of experience in quality assurance, trade facilitation, and industry development in the Indian textile sector.

Key Milestones

The journey of TEFC — from establishment to becoming a national textile export facilitation institution

2022 — Establishment
Launch

TEFC Officially Launched by MoT

The Ministry of Textiles launched TEFC as a flagship initiative under the Textiles Committee to serve as a one-stop export facilitation hub for textile MSMEs across India.

2022 — First Centres
Expansion

Karur & Surat Centres Inaugurated

The first two TEFCs became operational in Karur (Tamil Nadu) and Surat (Gujarat), providing services to over 300 exporters within the first six months of operation.

2023 — Full Network
Network

All 6 Cluster Centres Operational

Ichalkaranji, Jaipur, Varanasi, and Ludhiana centres completed the TEFC network, enabling comprehensive pan-India textile export facilitation services.

2023 — Digital Services
Technology

TEFC Online Portal Launched

The end-to-end digital services portal went live, enabling exporters to apply for certifications, access market data, and submit scheme applications from anywhere in India.

2023 — Policy Impact
Policy

RoDTEP Claims Facilitated for 500+ Exporters

TEFC successfully guided 500+ exporters in claiming RoDTEP benefits, collectively resulting in duty credit scrips worth over ₹120 crore being availed.

2024 — Trade Facilitation
Achievement

1st International Buyer-Seller Meet — 12 Countries

TEFC hosted its first large-scale international buyer-seller meet with 200+ exporters and buyers from 12 countries, generating over ₹450 crore in potential business leads.

2024 — Recognition
Recognition

TEFC Wins National Export Facilitation Award

The Ministry of Commerce recognised TEFC with the National Award for Best Export Facilitation Initiative for its impact on textile MSME competitiveness and export growth.

2025–26 — Future
Roadmap

₹100 Bn Export Target Initiative & Cluster 2.0

TEFC launches its ambitious Cluster 2.0 programme with upgraded infrastructure, AI-powered market intelligence, and dedicated sustainability certification pathways for all six clusters.
