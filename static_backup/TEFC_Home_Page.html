About Us

The Textiles Export and Facilitation Centre (TEFC) is a strategic initiative by the Textiles Committee, Ministry of Textiles, Govt. of India. We serve as a one-stop support hub for MSMEs and exporters in the textile sector, providing essential market intelligence, trade certification, and quality assurance to help Indian textile businesses compete globally.

Tamil Nadu

Karur

Weaving decorative home textiles — bed linens, kitchen linen, curtains & home decor.

Gujarat

Surat

Processing and exporting synthetic textiles — sarees, dress materials, and fabrics.

Maharashtra

Ichalkaranji

Supplying cotton yarn and fabrics — one of India's largest powerloom centres.

Rajasthan

Jaipur

Printing and dyeing colourful fabrics — renowned for block prints and ethnic textiles.

Uttar Pradesh

Varanasi

Traditional silk weaving — famous for Banarasi sarees with intricate zari work.

Punjab

Ludhiana

Knitting and woollen textiles — India's largest knitwear and hosiery manufacturing hub.

FTA Benefits

Government schemes and incentives to boost your textile exports

RoDTEP Scheme Active

  • Access to Duty Credit Scrips
  • Rebate on Central & State taxes

Eligibility: All textile exporters

PLI Scheme Active

  • Incentive up to ₹10 Cr for increased textile production
  • MMF apparel, MMF fabrics, Technical textiles

Eligibility: MMF, Technical Textiles Manufacturers

TUFS Active

  • 15% subsidy on capital investment for textile machinery upgradation

Eligibility: All textile units upgrading machinery
