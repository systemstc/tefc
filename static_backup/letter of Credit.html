1. What is a Letter of Credit?

A Letter of Credit (LC) is a financial instrument issued by a bank that guarantees payment to the exporter on behalf of the importer, provided that all the specified documents and conditions are fulfilled. It reduces payment risk and ensures trust between international trading partners.

2. Documents Required

  • Sales Contract / Purchase Agreement
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Insurance Certificate
  • Import Export Code (IEC)

3. How it Works

  1. Buyer requests LC from issuing bank
  2. Issuing bank sends LC to advising bank
  3. Exporter ships goods as per LC terms
  4. Exporter submits documents to bank
  5. Bank verifies and releases payment
  6. Importer receives documents to claim goods
